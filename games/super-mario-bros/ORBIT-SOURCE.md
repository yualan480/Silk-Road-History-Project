# Super Mario Bros. — user-supplied NES game

No Nintendo ROM is included or downloaded. Players load their own authorized .nes file. The emulator remembers that local file and saves emulator states in this browser's IndexedDB every two seconds and on pause/visibility changes. Saves are keyed by the ROM SHA-256; export/import is provided.

Emulator: JSNES 3.0.0, https://github.com/bfirsh/jsnes/tree/v3.0.0, Apache-2.0 (license retained in vendor/LICENSE). Bundled file from https://registry.npmjs.org/jsnes/-/jsnes-3.0.0.tgz .
