TETRISDUEL

Two players, the same seven-piece bags. Fast drops and a 90-second clock keep every duel moving.

How to play
  • Blocks start dropping every 0.24 seconds and accelerate
  • Clear 2, 3 or 4 lines to send garbage to your rival
  • Top out and your opponent wins
  • Still standing after 90 seconds? Higher score wins
Computer skill

Arrows / A and D move. Up rotates, Down soft drops, Space hard drops, C holds. Touch controls are below your board. Online play needs a connection to the matchmaking server.
