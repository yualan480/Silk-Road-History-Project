# Unblocked Games fonts

Locally hosted Latin variable-font subsets, unmodified files delivered by Google Fonts:

- Oxanium, weights 400–700, titles: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/oxanium/v21/RrQQboN_4yJ0JmiMe2LE0Q.woff2
- Outfit, weights 400–600, descriptions/interface text: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/outfit/v15/QGYvz_MVcBeNP4NJtEtq.woff2

Both are SIL Open Font License 1.1 fonts. Original copyright/license notices are retained in Oxanium-OFL.txt and Outfit-OFL.txt. Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oxanium and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/outfit . Fonts are used only in the surrounding Orbit gallery/player UI, not inside embedded games.
